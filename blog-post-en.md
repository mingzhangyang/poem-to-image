## Common Text Error Scenarios

Text issues in AI image generation typically occur in these situations:

1. **Poems and Quotes**: Classical poetry or famous sayings are most prone to errors
2. **Branded Content**: Company names, slogans, or product descriptions require 100% accuracy
3. **Multilingual Content**: Non-English text rendering often has more issues
4. **Artistic Typography**: Decorative text layout and readability are hard to guarantee

These problems stem from AI models' training data and generation mechanisms: AI excels at understanding overall visual concepts but struggles with precise text typography and spelling control.
